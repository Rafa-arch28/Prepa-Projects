The Peacock Turtle

What are they?

Peacock turtles are semi-aquatic reptiles known for the colors and patterns on their shells. They mainly live in rivers, lagoons, and tropical areas of South America.

They need both a space with water for swimming and a dry area where they can rest and receive light.

Curiosities

  • Their shells can have patterns that resemble the eyes of a peacock.
  • They are excellent swimmers and spend much of their time in the water.
  • Like other reptiles, they depend on their environment to regulate their temperature.
  • They can live for many years when they receive proper food and care.

How to get one

Before getting a peacock turtle, find out whether keeping one is allowed in your area. Only get one from a legal breeder or store that can prove its origin.

Never buy animals captured from their natural habitat or release them into rivers or lagoons. It is also important to prepare their space and have the necessary budget before bringing one home.

Care for a healthy life

  • Provide a large aquarium with clean water and a dry area for resting.
  • Use a suitable filter and perform partial water changes regularly.
  • Keep a stable temperature and use specialized reptile lighting.
  • Offer a varied diet recommended by a specialized veterinarian.
  • Watch their behavior, appetite, and shell for changes.

Advice

Wash your hands after touching the turtle or cleaning its space and avoid handling it unnecessarily. Turtles can carry bacteria that affect people, so hygiene is essential.

If you notice that it stops eating, has trouble breathing, or has changes in its shell, consult an exotic-animal veterinarian and do not try to medicate it yourself.
